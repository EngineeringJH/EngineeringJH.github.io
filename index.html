Robotic Gripper Mechanism

This mechanism was developed for automated pick-and-place tasks. The goal was to minimize part count while retaining high torsional rigidity under continuous load.

Design Specifications

  • Material: 6061-T6 Aluminum (CNC) & PETG (Prototype)
  • Actuation: NEMA 17 Stepper via lead screw
  • Payload Capacity: 1.2 kg
